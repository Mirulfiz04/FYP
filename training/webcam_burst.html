📸 MEDS Face Burst Capture

1) Pick an emotion. 2) Make that face. 3) Hold "Capture" and slowly move your head a little (turn left/right, tilt, closer/farther) so you get varied shots. Aim for ~150–250 images per emotion. 4) Click Download — the files save named like happy_....jpg. Everything runs locally in your browser.
